## Status legend

- **[V] Verified:** bibliographic fact, table of contents, abstract, or quoted framework checked against a fetched publisher, author, scholarly venue, or library source.
- **[R] Reconstructed:** new prose based on model knowledge and the verified structure; not checked line by line against the original full text.
- **[S] Synthesized:** an explicit cross-source editorial connection or workflow not claimed by one source.
- **[U] Unverified:** a detail could not be verified from a fetched primary or publisher source during this pass.

No file in this corpus is a substitute for the original books or papers. No long passage is reproduced; chapter entries are concise summaries.

## Attribution and edition corrections

- **[V]** *The Gamer's Brain* is by **Celia Hodent**, released in 2017; the suggested Pichlmair/Johnson attribution was not supported by publisher or author sources. (*The Gamer's Brain*, Celia Hodent, 2017.)
- **[V]** *A Playful Production Process* is by **Richard Lemarchand**, published in 2021; the suggested Kremers attribution was not supported by MIT Press. (*A Playful Production Process*, Richard Lemarchand, 2021.)
- **[V]** O'Reilly dates *The Art of Game Design*, third edition, to July 2019, while Routledge displays copyright 2020; this corpus uses 2019 and preserves the discrepancy. (*The Art of Game Design*, Jesse Schell, 3rd ed., 2019; copyright 2020.)
- **[V]** Pearson lists *Advanced Game Design* as published 2017-10-30 with copyright 2018; this corpus uses 2017 as publication year. (*Advanced Game Design*, Michael Sellers, 2017; copyright 2018.)
- **[V]** ScienceDirect lists *Game Feel* as published 2008 and displays copyright 2009; this corpus uses 2008 as publication year. (*Game Feel*, Steve Swink, 2008.)

## Explicit unverified/model-knowledge markers

- **[R]** Every book’s concept explanation is reconstructed unless the file says “publisher-verified summary”; tables of contents do not verify an interpretation.
- **[U]** The exact original 2017 chapter wording and numbering for every late chapter of *The Gamer's Brain* was only partially available from original-edition samples; the two-part arc and subject order are verified, but the file should be checked against a physical or licensed 2017 copy before formal bibliographic use.
- **[U]** Csikszentmihalyi’s original 1975 and 1990 books were not fetched in full; the flow file marks those passages reconstructed and relies on standard bibliographic corroboration plus verified game-application papers.
- **[R]** The *Blood, Sweat, and Pixels* case summaries are entirely model-knowledge reconstructions anchored to a verified chapter list; they should not be used as historical evidence without returning to Schreier’s text.
- **[S]** The causal stack, cross-source bridges, question routes, design workflows, and critical comparisons are original synthesis for this canon map, not consensus statements attributed to the cited authors.

## Source-access boundary

Web access was available and used for publisher pages, author pages, official tables of contents, licensed previews, primary open-access papers, and library catalogs. Full licensed text was not available for most books, so the corpus deliberately avoids pretending that a verified table of contents is a verified chapter summary.
